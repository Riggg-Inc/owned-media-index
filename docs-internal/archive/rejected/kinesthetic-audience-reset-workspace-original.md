# Kinesthetic Audience Reset

## What It Is

A technique using lightweight physical motions to relight audience brains and break presentation monotony.

## Best For

Presentations and large group settings where maintaining audience engagement and focus is critical. This pattern is effective when attention spans may be waning or to break up long periods of passive listening.

## Why It Works

This pattern works by introducing a physical novelty that breaks the monotony of "resting business face," effectively "relighting" the brains of listeners. It provides presenters with immediate, non-verbal feedback on audience engagement without requiring verbal disruption, helping to re-engage the audience and improve comprehension.

## Quality Bar

The successful application of this pattern is marked by a noticeable shift in audience demeanor, demonstrating renewed engagement, improved focus, and increased responsiveness following the kinesthetic cue.
