# Developing Sequenced 'As Time Goes On' Messaging

## What It Is

Developing a series of "as time goes on" talking points ensures each touch remains fresh and relevant in executive outreach, preventing fatigue and reducing the time to get a response.

## Best For

- Executive outreach
- High-value prospect engagement
- Complex sales cycles

## Why It Works

Executives require multiple touches, but repeating the same value proposition causes disconnect and fatigue. By evolving the message over time, each interaction provides new value and maintains engagement, driving higher response rates.

## Required Elements

- Defined sequence of messaging touchpoints.
- Unique value proposition or insight for each touch.
- Tracking mechanism for message sequence and executive responses.

## Quality Bar

Each message in the sequence should feel fresh, relevant, and build upon previous interactions without repeating information. The sequence should lead naturally to the desired call to action.

## When Not To Use

Avoid heavy real-time production when:

- One-off or transactional outreach where a single clear message is sufficient.
- Low-stakes communication where message fatigue is not a primary concern.
