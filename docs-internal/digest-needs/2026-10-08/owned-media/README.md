This repairs the contradictions with the least conceptual change.

This is more literal, but requires a broader terminology change throughout the Index. No such rewrite is prepared or authorized here.

## Source-to-claim evidence

All network retrieval was ordinary public web_fetch, no access bypass. Saved JSON captures URLs, actual fetch times, HTTP status and truncation flags. The longer Transistor/YouTube spill files are retained. Claims below use complete relevant sections and surrounding context, not search snippets.

1. **U.S. Copyright Office, What is Copyright?** source-0.json, HTTP 200. “Original Works” requires human originality/minimal creativity and explicitly excludes titles, names, short phrases and slogans. “Who is a copyright owner?” explains employee works made for hire, certain commissioned works and transfers/assignments. “When can I use works that are not mine?” distinguishes licenses, statutory exceptions and public-domain use. Supports qualifying creation-based ownership and identifying the inventory as operational, not a catalogue of individually copyrighted objects. U.S.-specific; does not adjudicate any project contract or other jurisdiction.
2. **Copyright Office, Title 17 Chapter 2** source-1.json, HTTP 200. Sections 201(a)-(d) distinguish authorship, joint ownership, work made for hire and transfer; section 202 expressly separates copyright ownership from possession of the material object; section 204(a) requires a signed writing for ownership transfers other than operation of law. Supports not equating file possession or a use license with ownership. The draft does not turn these rules into customer-specific legal advice. Checking term/territory/editing permissions is recommended contract diligence, not a claim that all contracts contain identical restrictions.
3. **Transistor Terms** source-2.json and transistor-full.txt, HTTP 200. “Account suspension and deletion” permits sole-discretion suspension/deletion without notice for accounts deemed inappropriate, offensive or in violation. “Content provided by Users” requires lawful upload rights; “Rights regarding content provided by Users” grants a non-exclusive, fully paid-up, royalty-free license solely for operation/maintenance as contractually required. “Liability for provided content” separately permits removal/access denial for complaints, infringement notices, authority orders or risks. The draft quotes the narrower account condition, not an invented right to suspend for any reason, and makes no claim of guaranteed export after suspension.
4. **YouTube Terms, dated Dec 15 2023 in retrieved copy** source-4.json and youtube-full.txt, HTTP 200. “Uploading Content” requires permission or other legal entitlement for third-party IP. “Rights you Grant” retains existing ownership while granting platform/user licenses. “Reservation” does not give service ownership. “Develop, Improve and Update” permits changes with notice qualifications. “Account Suspension & Termination” lists material/repeated breach, legal requirement/court order, or reasonably believed liability/harm, with notice exceptions. Supports separating rights from platform reach, not the false claim that all independent audience relationships disappear.
5. **Google Search Central, AI features and your website** source-5.json, HTTP 200. “Technical requirements” requires indexing and snippet eligibility, no additional AI technical requirements. “SEO best practices” says specific AI optimization is not required. Supports preserving searchable assets without promising AI inclusion or treating vector indexing/LLM-specific show notes as a requirement.
6. **PESO first-party retrieval unresolved.** Both https://spinsucks.com/communication/peso-model/ and https://spinsucks.com/the-peso-model/ returned HTTP 403; errors saved. Search provider also failed; no snippets used. Historical Oct 7 report records earlier 403 attempts. Removed the section’s PESO name, 2014 date, attribution and comparison from draft/frontmatter rather than cite inaccessible evidence. The operational asset/channel distinction is retained as Riggg’s proposed definition, not presented as a PESO authority claim.
