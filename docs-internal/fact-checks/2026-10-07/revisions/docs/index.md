# Owned Media Index

The Owned Media Index (OMI) is an open library of patterns, tools, benchmarks, and standards for turning expert conversations into published owned media. Use it to plan, produce, package, publish, measure, and reuse your content. Maintained by Riggg. Open to the community.

## Why This Exists

We built this index to bring practical guidance and its evidence into one place.

This index is opinionated. Our editorial standard asks each pattern to answer three questions:

1. **What is this for?** — Use case and context.
2. **When do you use it?** — Trigger conditions, skill level, format.
3. **What does good look like?** — Quality bar, benchmark, or minimum standard.

Scores are editorial judgments. Our [scoring standard](scoring.md) reserves 5 for a recommended default with strong evidence and broad usefulness; consult each entry's evidence and limitations.
