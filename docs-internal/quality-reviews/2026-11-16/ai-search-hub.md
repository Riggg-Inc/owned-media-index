## reasonable_claims: needs_revision

This does not prove that every search crawler processes audio; recommend readable text without claiming general AI incapacity. Google's complete AI-features guidance says indexing and snippet eligibility are required for supporting links, no special AI markup is required, and crawling/indexing/serving are not guaranteed. This conflicts with a universal mandatory stack and permanence promise. The fastest-growing discovery-channel assertion and blanket account of what traditional podcast agencies do have no cited substantiation here; remove or scope these specific comparisons, not all confident advice. Terminology varies across the market: present this taxonomy as this Index's working definitions, not a universal standard.
