- Support: Do not substitute lower-level metrics for higher-level outcomes. - Locators: Sections 2–3, pp.8–10; Sections 5.2–5.4.4, pp.13–20; Sections 6.1–6.4, pp.21–25; Section 8, pp.27–29
- Support: Server-side download delivery is not confirmed playback. ## M5: Measure key moments for audience retention

- Organization: Google / YouTube
- URL: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en
- Publication: Unknown; not inferred
- Actual research retrieval: 2026-10-08T21:10:18.453659Z
- Coverage: Complete HTML response fetched (1,625,222 bytes); full article text read including graph guidance and segment definitions.
- Locators: Understand key moments; Intros / Spikes / Dips; View audience retention by segment type; View detailed activity
- Support: Retention generally takes 1–2 days to process and is video-level. Compare similar-length videos; inspect intro, top moments, spikes and dips. A spike can signal either interest/rewatching or confusion; a dip can be skipping or abandonment. Highlighted moments require detection and a video at least 60 seconds long with at least 100 views. Segment organic/paid and new/returning audiences where available.
- Limits: Platform diagnostic observations, not causal proof of why viewers leave or a universal retention benchmark. Highlight eligibility is not a statistical significance threshold.
- Mapping: Attach timestamps to an editorial improvement hypothesis; do not convert a graph spike into demonstrated understanding.

## M6: Analytics

- Organization: Apple
- URL: https://podcasters.apple.com/support/5392-listener-analytics
- Publication: Unknown; not inferred
- Actual research retrieval: 2026-10-08T21:08:59.362Z
- Coverage: Complete extracted page including episode metrics and performance comparisons.
- Locators: Opening overview; Show Analytics; Episodes; Performance
- Support: Apple reports aggregated listening/viewing completion based on unique devices, distinct from host downloads. Engaged listeners heard/watched at least 20 minutes or 40% of an episode. Average consumption is average percentage per device and can exceed 100% with repeat listening. Performance compares equal days since release (7, 14, 30 or 60) with median/average/top baselines. At least five unique listeners must play show content for selected-period data to appear.
- Limits: Apple audience only; not all podcast listeners. Five-listener threshold is data availability, not representative-sample adequacy. Show followers are not automatically attributable to one episode.
- Mapping: Use episode listeners and consumption, label engaged-listener denominator, compare equal release ages, mark unavailable data rather than zero.

- Support: Attribution assigns credit under a rule or model to touchpoints. Models differ; reporting model, eligible channels and lookback window are settings.
