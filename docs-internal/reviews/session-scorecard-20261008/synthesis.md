## Deliberate usability and measurement choices

- Pre-release readiness, actual-release checks, outcome review, immediate preservation and later reuse are separate moments. No universal window or threshold, composite score or weighting.
- Source context, caption correction, full versus sampled playback and permission restrictions fit into existing evidence references. These are not a full legal/accessibility review.
- Example IDs/names/events/counts and relative days are explicitly fictional. 12/100 uses sessions with at least one click and the same consented episode-page session denominator/window. It is not a people or inquiry rate. No cross-platform reach sum, causal exit diagnosis or growth claim.
- Unknown/thresholded/uncollected and available zero differ. No baseline is allowed. Tracked association and model credit are not incremental causation.
- Markdown is transported as .md.txt with download filename .md; MkDocs therefore serves a raw copyable file without building a second article or requiring config/hook changes. CSV is a small four-column form, not a massive metric-row schema. Both have one header, five checks, three evidence slots (third optional), one decision and short instructions.
