# Scope-first intake — owner direction, 2026-10-06

Before creating any candidate, grounded finding, revision or duplicate, and before Scribe drafts or Auditor advances one:

1. Read the full relevant source passage and surrounding context, not a search hit/snippet. Identify source modality, participants, who did what, what actually happened and what is merely the interviewed topic. A topic discussed ON a show is not evidence of a technique used TO produce/distribute it. Source counts and decisive primary legal text cannot substitute for scope. Preserve existing access, evidence labels, scoring and source-count policies; apply those after scope.
2. Name the reusable recorded-media asset (or its owned supporting newsletter, episode page or library), its Produce/Package/Publish/Prove/Preserve stage, concrete medium practice, and audience outcome tied to production, packaging, distribution, measurement or preservation. Do not launder an unrelated mechanism by appending owned-media language.
3. Training/L&D design, cohort routing, facilitated audience games, keynote facilitation, sales prospecting, programmatic monetization and legal guides are outside current scope. Educational/B2B subject matter alone is NOT a rejection: genuine recorded educational media, relevant guest preparation, recurring recorded segments, AI-assisted naming/packaging, owned newsletters/pages and library management remain eligible with actual medium-practice evidence. Teardowns remain a separate held initiative.
4. Check identifiers AND semantic intent against `docs-internal/intake-suppressions.json`, both archive READMEs, and current existing/retired/superseded Workboard cards. Renaming a rejected mechanism does not make it new. Existing eligible matches receive contextual evidence, not another candidate. Never automatically retire other backlog.
5. Complete `schemas/scope-evidence.schema.json` as a public-safe record attached to the card (private passage stays in its authorized source, referenced by locator; no private transcript dumps). Run `python3 scripts/scope_preflight.py /absolute/path/to/record.json` from the current repo checkout before filing/drafting/advancing. Retain the record and verdict. Missing/full-context uncertain evidence returns for context. Borderlines become a concise watch item on the role charter, not a candidate flood. Ask the owner only about a genuinely new boundary, not already rejected classes.
6. Scribe and both Auditor passes refuse to draft/advance without a complete record, passing deterministic preflight AND semantic context review. A pass is not owner approval or publication authorization. Existing approval/public-safety/voice/NO_REPLY contracts remain unchanged.

## Enforcement limits

The CLI validates declared structured evidence, blocked mechanism categories and known identifiers. It cannot verify truthful classification, retrieve full sources, infer semantic duplicates or intercept generic Workboard tools. Those gates remain agent-enforced. CI blocks known retired slugs in publication/source trees and checks archive hashes; there is no impossible-to-bypass intake interceptor. Runtime scheduler payloads must explicitly call this procedure; charter comments alone do not guarantee execution.
