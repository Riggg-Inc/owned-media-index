## Five checks

Use checked / needs fixing / not checked / doesn't apply, plus a note or link. No points or total grade. For Prove, write what you learned or what is missing.

- **Produce:** Is the recording usable for the content we planned? [Status + note/link]
- **Package:** Are edits accurate, captions checked and permissions sorted? [Status + note/link]
- **Publish:** Is the right version live, with working playback and links? [Status + note/link]
- **Prove:** What do the results tell us about our goal? [Note, or what's missing]
- **Preserve:** Can a teammate find and open the recording, final edits and transcript? [Status + folder link]

## Two useful results

**Result 1:**
- What happened?
- Where did we find it? [Report/link and dates]
- What should we check or change?

**Result 2:**
- What happened?
- Where did we find it? [Report/link and dates]
- What should we check or change?

Add a third result only if it changes your decision. Audience questions or feedback can count as a result. If data isn't available, say why; don't write zero. Compare similar episodes at the same time after publication. When using a percentage, include the counts behind it from the same group and dates.

Check recording, edits and permissions before release; test playback and links once published. Mark what you actually checked, including whether playback was full or sampled. Fix release problems separately from judging audience response. Keep usage restrictions with saved files and recheck them before reuse. Link private reports rather than copying personal details into this sheet.

A viewing dip is a clue, not proof of its cause. Clicks aren't inquiries; downloads aren't confirmed listening. This review doesn't replace production checks or prove that an episode caused a sale.
