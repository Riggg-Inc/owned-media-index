# Instagram Caption

An Instagram episode-announcement caption introduces the release through its visual and one reason to care. Use it with an episode asset and a clear listening or viewing action; make the first line meaningful on its own.

**Stage:** Package → Social Posts → Episode Announcement  
**Score:** 4  
**Evidence:** practitioner observation

## Platform

Instagram

## What It Is

An Instagram episode-announcement caption introduces the release through its visual and one reason to care.

## When To Use

Use it with an episode asset and a clear listening or viewing action; make the first line meaningful on its own.

## Template

Start with the actual episode visual and one reason your audience should care. Choose the destination before writing the CTA.


description of the visual → one useful episode insight → one listening/viewing action

## Quality Bar

- The first line gives a specific reason to care, beyond the episode title.
- The caption supports what the image or video actually shows.
- The insight accurately reflects the episode; quotes match the source.
- One CTA leads to a destination the reader can actually find. Check it in the published profile or post flow.
- Preview line breaks and truncation in the current app; keep tags relevant.

## Example

*Hypothetical episode and caption, not a real guest quotation or measured result.*

Visual: an episode card titled “Keep the answer, cut the setup.” The episode demonstrates an editing check: play a clip without its preceding interview question.

> Your best answer may need less setup, not a bigger headline.
>
> In this episode, we take one interview answer and test whether it makes sense without the host’s question. If it does not, keep the context that makes the answer clear.
>
> Watch “Keep the answer, cut the setup” through the episode link in our bio.

Use that CTA only after placing the matching episode destination in the profile. Choose a different, accurate route when that is not your setup.

## Prompt Template

Copy and customize:


- audience: [who this episode helps]
- episode: [title and accurate summary]
- visual: [describe the actual accompanying image/video]
- source insight: [verified episode point or exact quotation]
- public guest details: [approved name/role/handle, if relevant]
- viewing/listening route: [the destination and how readers reach it]

Write an Instagram episode-announcement caption. Open with one reason to care, connect it to the actual visual and episode insight, then give one CTA using the supplied route. Use only supplied facts; do not invent quotations, results or visual details. Keep it within an editorial drafting target of 2200 characters, use readable line breaks and preview the final caption in the current app. Include tags only when relevant. Return the caption, followed by any missing inputs separately.

Check the draft against the Quality Bar before publishing.
