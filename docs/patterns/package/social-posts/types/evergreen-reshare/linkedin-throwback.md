# Linkedin Throwback

A LinkedIn throwback post resurfaces an older episode through a lesson still relevant to a professional audience. Give a current reason to revisit it rather than presenting an old release as new.

**Stage:** Package → Social Posts → Evergreen Reshare  
**Score:** 4  
**Evidence:** practitioner observation

## Platform

LinkedIn

## What It Is

A LinkedIn throwback post resurfaces an older episode through a lesson still relevant to a professional audience.

## When To Use

Give a current reason to revisit it rather than presenting an old release as new.

## Template

```
[Hook — first line earns the second line]

[Body — value, context, or story]

[CTA — one clear action]

[Tags/Hashtags if appropriate]
```

## Quality Bar

- Identify the older episode without calling it new.
- Explain a present-day reason to revisit the lesson.
- Recheck any dated claims before quoting them.
- First line must stop the scroll
- Body must add value beyond the episode title
- CTA must be specific and singular
- Platform-native formatting (line breaks, emojis, hashtags per platform norms)

## Example

Fictional example, not a measured result:

> Revisiting a useful lesson from our June episode: document who owns the next step before a project handoff.
>
> If your team is planning a new quarter, try adding an owner and due date to one handoff this week. That is the specific practice our fictional guest described—not a claim that every team needs a new process.
>
> Listen to the original conversation: [episode URL].

## Prompt Template

Copy and customize this prompt to generate this pattern:

```
Write a Evergreen Reshare social post for LinkedIn (Linkedin Throwback version).

Context:
- Original publication date: [date]
- Why this lesson matters now: [current reader problem]
- Accuracy check: [what you rechecked; omit stale specifics]
- Episode title: [title of the episode]
- Guest name: [guest full name]
- Guest company/title: [their role]
- Key insight or quote: [the main moment to highlight]
- Episode link: [URL]
- Relevant hashtags: [industry hashtags if appropriate]

Platform: LinkedIn
Drafting target: about 1300 characters; an editorial target, not a platform limit or proven engagement optimum

Requirements:
- First line must stop the scroll
- Body must add value beyond the episode title
- CTA must be specific and singular
- Platform-native formatting (line breaks, emojis, hashtags per LinkedIn norms)
- Tag guest when appropriate
- Never use the same copy across platforms
```

Replace the bracketed placeholders with your specific details. The more context you provide about your audience, guest, and episode content, the better the output.
