# Linkedin Gratitude

A LinkedIn gratitude post thanks a guest by highlighting their specific contribution to the episode. Tie appreciation to a useful idea; a guest tag alone is not a reason to read.

**Stage:** Package → Social Posts → Guest Tag  
**Score:** 4  
**Evidence:** practitioner observation

## Platform

LinkedIn

## What It Is

A LinkedIn gratitude post thanks a guest by highlighting their specific contribution to the episode.

## When To Use

Tie appreciation to a useful idea; a guest tag alone is not a reason to read.

## Template

```
[Hook — first line earns the second line]

[Body — value, context, or story]

[CTA — one clear action]

[Tags/Hashtags if appropriate]
```

## Quality Bar

- Name the contribution, not just the guest.
- Verify the guest profile before selecting the mention.
- Make the post useful even if the guest never reshares it.
- First line must stop the scroll
- Body must add value beyond the episode title
- CTA must be specific and singular
- Platform-native formatting (line breaks, emojis, hashtags per platform norms)

## Example

Fictional example; replace the bracketed mention with the verified guest profile:

> Thank you, [guest], for making project handoffs concrete in our conversation.
>
> Your example—write down the next action and its owner before a meeting ends—gives team leads something to try in their next planning session.
>
> Listen to the full discussion: [episode URL].

## Prompt Template

Copy and customize this prompt to generate this pattern:

```
Write a Guest Tag social post for LinkedIn (Linkedin Gratitude version).

Context:
- Contribution to thank the guest for: [specific explanation or example]
- Verified guest profile: [profile to select when posting]
- Reader application: [what the audience can do with the idea]
- Episode title: [title of the episode]
- Guest name: [guest full name]
- Guest company/title: [their role]
- Key insight or quote: [the main moment to highlight]
- Episode link: [URL]
- Relevant hashtags: [industry hashtags if appropriate]

Platform: LinkedIn
Drafting target: about 1300 characters; an editorial target, not a platform limit or proven engagement optimum

Requirements:
- First line must stop the scroll
- Body must add value beyond the episode title
- CTA must be specific and singular
- Platform-native formatting (line breaks, emojis, hashtags per LinkedIn norms)
- Tag guest when appropriate
- Never use the same copy across platforms
```

Replace the bracketed placeholders with your specific details. The more context you provide about your audience, guest, and episode content, the better the output.
