# Twitter Quote Card

A Twitter/X quote-card post pairs an attributed quotation graphic with short supporting copy. Use a readable, self-contained quote and clear attribution rather than anonymous advice.

**Stage:** Package → Social Posts → Quote Share  
**Score:** 4  
**Evidence:** practitioner observation

## Platform

Twitter/X

## What It Is

A Twitter/X quote-card post pairs an attributed quotation graphic with short supporting copy.

## When To Use

Use a readable, self-contained quote and clear attribution rather than anonymous advice.

## Template

```
[Graphic — one exact, self-contained quote]
[Attribution — speaker name and episode]
[Caption — why this quote matters to the reader]
[CTA — episode link and one next action]
```

## Quality Bar

- The quote matches the recording or transcript word for word; paraphrases are not in quotation marks.
- The speaker and source are clear, and the graphic is readable on a phone.
- The caption adds context rather than repeating the image.
- Check the final caption in the composer, including the link.

## Example

**Illustrative example — invented quotation, not a real guest endorsement.**

Graphic: “Check the recording before everyone leaves.” — Example speaker, recording checklist episode.

Caption: A short playback check gives you a chance to catch missing audio while the guest is still available. Hear the full checklist: [episode link].

For a real card, replace this with an exact approved quotation and verified attribution.

## Prompt Template

Start with the actual source passage, not only the episode title.

```
Create a Twitter/X quote-card brief and caption from the source below.

Inputs:
- Intended reader and question: [reader + question]
- Episode title and public link: [title + verified URL]
- Source passage with speaker and timestamps: [paste relevant transcript]
- Public-use permissions and details to omit: [notes]

Output:
- Exact quotation, attribution, graphic layout brief and short caption.
- Suggest a paraphrase separately if the quote needs context; do not present it as verbatim.
- Give a source location for every quotation or substantive point in a separate review checklist.
- If the source lacks a needed fact, mark it missing; do not invent facts, steps or results.
- Keep any qualification that changes the meaning.
- Adapt the wording to this reader, not a copy of another platform post.
```

Before publishing, check each point against the recording, confirm names and links, and remove private or unapproved details. Fix mismatches and recheck; publish only when the remaining issues are resolved.

For ordinary short posts, [X counts weighted characters](https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/counting-characters), including 23 characters per URL; the composer is the final check.
