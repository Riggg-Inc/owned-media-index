# Riggg Quadrants

Riggg Quadrants compare selected patterns, recording methods, mastering tools, and distribution platforms on two decision axes. Use them to narrow a choice by audience, effort, capability, or ownership, then read the linked guidance and evidence.

Dots on the pattern and tool quadrants are color-coded by Riggg Score (teal = 5, green = 4, gold = 3). Dots on the distribution quadrant are color-coded by role (Anchor, Hybrid, Amplifier, Directory) and are labels only. Hover to see a name; linked dots open related guidance. Use each page’s selection guide to compare options without relying on the chart.
