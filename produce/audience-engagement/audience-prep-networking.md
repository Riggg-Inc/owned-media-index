# Pre-Session Alignment Check-ins

## What It Is
Hosts conducting brief alignment or pre-networking check-ins with attendees or guests before a session. This involves agreeing on the "big idea" or having a short discussion to set expectations and foster a collaborative environment.

## Best For
Sessions requiring high audience participation, interactive webinars, workshops, or any owned media event where audience contribution is critical for success.

## Why It Works
Pre-session alignment helps attendees and guests feel more comfortable, reduces tentativeness, and ensures everyone is on the same page regarding the session's objectives. This leads to significantly stronger in-session contributions and better overall audience alignment.

## Required Elements
*   A dedicated time slot (even brief) before the main session for alignment.
*   Clear communication of the session's "big idea" or core objective.
*   Opportunity for attendees/guests to ask questions or share initial thoughts.

## Quality Bar
Evidence of increased audience participation and positive feedback regarding clarity and engagement. Metrics could include higher Q&A engagement, more active chat participation, or richer discussion segments.

## When Not To Use
Very short, broadcast-style content where audience interaction is minimal or not a primary goal.
