# Facilitated Peer Connection

## What It Is
Explicitly designing moments within a session or event where the audience is encouraged to connect with one another, shifting the focus from expert-led content to horizontal community building.

## Best For
- Events focused on long-term audience retention.
- Programs aiming to build strong community around content.
- Sessions where participant engagement and networking are key.

## Why It Works
While high-quality vertical content initially attracts audiences, long-term retention and engagement rely heavily on fostering community connections among participants. By actively facilitating peer interaction, programs can accelerate this shift, making events more sticky and valuable.

## Required Elements
Not specified in card notes.

## Quality Bar
Successful implementation results in observable peer interaction and positive feedback regarding networking opportunities. The shift from passive consumption to active community participation should be evident.

## When Not To Use
- Sessions where deep, uninterrupted focus on expert content is paramount.
- Programs with extremely limited time where every minute must be dedicated to core curriculum delivery.
- Audiences who explicitly prefer purely one-way information transfer.

## Riggg Score
3

## Evidence
Practitioner observation. Based on production experience and expert judgment where explicitly designing moments for peer interaction was observed to accelerate community building and retention.
