# Low-Stakes Warmup Framing

## What It Is

Explicitly setting expectations for initial recording/interview questions as "throwaway warmups" to reduce guest anxiety and encourage natural performance.

## Best For

- Interviews with guests prone to on-camera anxiety.
- Sessions requiring authentic and energetic guest contributions.
- Podcast or video productions where early rapport is critical.

## Why It Works

By lowering the perceived stakes of initial interactions, guests feel permission to be unpolished, reducing self-consciousness and allowing them to relax into their natural demeanor. This leads to more genuine and vibrant performances earlier in the session.

## Required Elements

- Clear verbal communication to the guest about the "warmup" nature of initial questions.
- Reassurance that early segments might not be used in the final edit.
- A genuine and supportive producer/host demeanor.

## Quality Bar

The guest should visibly relax and their performance should become more natural and engaging within the "warmup" period.

## When Not To Use

Avoid heavy real-time production when:

- When strict adherence to a precise script is required from the very beginning.
- When the production timeline does not allow for a "warmup" period.
