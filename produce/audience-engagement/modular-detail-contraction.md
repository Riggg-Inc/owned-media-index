
# Modular Detail Contraction

## What It Is

Rather than building monolithic slide decks that force a rigid run-of-show, successful presenters establish three fixed key messages but treat the supporting details (like case studies or tactical data) as an accordion. They expand or contract these details based on audience questions and engagement in real time, ensuring they never run out of time for the critical soundbite ending.

## Best For

- Presentations requiring real-time adaptation.
- Engaging audiences with varied interests.
- Workshops or interactive sessions.
- Avoiding time overruns in presentations.

## Why It Works

It allows presenters to maintain core messaging while dynamically responding to audience needs and time constraints, preventing information overload and keeping engagement high.

## Required Elements

- Pre-defined core messages (e.g., 3 key takeaways).
- Categorized supporting details for flexible expansion/contraction.
- Presenter training in real-time adaptation.

## Quality Bar

Presenters can fluidly adjust detail depth without losing coherence or exceeding allocated time, and audience feedback indicates high engagement.

## When Not To Use

- Highly structured, non-interactive presentations.
- Situations where all details are mandatory and cannot be condensed.
- Presenters new to public speaking who require a strict script.
