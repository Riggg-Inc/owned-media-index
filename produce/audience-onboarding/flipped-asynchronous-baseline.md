# Flipped Asynchronous Baseline

## What It Is

A pattern for delivering foundational concepts asynchronously *before* live, human-led sessions to ensure consistent baseline knowledge and prevent the spread of misunderstandings.

## Best For

- Learning and training cohorts.
- Programs where foundational concepts are critical.
- Scenarios where preventing the spread of "bad habits" is important.

## Why It Works

By front-loading standardized foundational content asynchronously, participants acquire a consistent baseline. Live sessions can then focus on reinforcement, application, and deeper engagement, mitigating the risk of misunderstandings propagating through peer-to-peer live instruction.

## Required Elements

- Standardized asynchronous content (e.g., videos, readings, interactive modules).
- Clear expectation-setting for participants to complete asynchronous work.
- Live sessions structured for reinforcement and application, not initial concept delivery.

## Quality Bar

Participants arrive at live sessions with a consistent, verified understanding of foundational concepts, enabling immediate higher-level engagement.

## When Not To Use

Avoid this pattern when:

- The session is highly exploratory or unstructured, and foundational knowledge is less critical.
- The primary goal is spontaneous, organic discovery without a set curriculum.
