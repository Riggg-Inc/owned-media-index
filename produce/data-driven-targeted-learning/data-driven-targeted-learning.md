# Data-Driven Targeted Learning

## What It Is

Routing individuals into specific learning tracks based on baseline engagement survey metrics to address individual survey deficits.

## Best For

- Programs aiming for measurable improvement in audience understanding or skill.
- Organizations moving away from generalized learning approaches.
- Content creators seeking higher recorded engagement scores.

## Why It Works

By focusing on individual needs identified through data, this approach enables measurable pre- and post-program tracking, leading to clearer improvements and higher engagement. It avoids wasting resources on information already known or irrelevant to the individual.

## Required Elements

- Baseline engagement survey metrics.
- Targeted learning tracks or content modules.
- System for routing individuals based on survey deficits.
- Pre- and post-program tracking mechanisms.

## Quality Bar

Demonstrable improvement in target metrics for individuals completing a track. High correlation between identified deficits and engagement with targeted content.

## When Not To Use

- When a broad, general awareness is the primary goal, rather than specific skill improvement.
- When resources for granular content creation and individual tracking are limited.
- For exploratory sessions where flexible learning paths are preferred.

3

## Evidence

Evidence level: practitioner-observation (single-session finding with quantitative survey impact). Generalized from internal program data.
