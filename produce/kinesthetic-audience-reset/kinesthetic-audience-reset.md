# Kinesthetic Audience Reset

## What It Is

Using lightweight physical motions ("kinesthetic engagement") relights audience brains and breaks presentation monotony, preventing resting "business face" and providing non-verbal engagement signals without stopping the flow.

## Best For

- Presentations and sessions where audience engagement can wane.
- Long-form content requiring sustained attention.
- Virtual events to combat "business face" and provide non-verbal engagement signals.

## Why It Works

Brief physical movements help audiences re-engage by shifting focus and breaking static postures, improving retention and perceived energy without disrupting flow.

## Required Elements

- Planned moments for physical activity.
- Simple, easy-to-follow movements.
- Clear instructions for the audience.

## Quality Bar

Motions should be simple, inclusive, and not distract from the core message. They should feel natural and enhance engagement.

## When Not To Use

Avoid when:

- Physical movement is inappropriate for the audience or setting.
- In highly formal or sensitive presentations where it might be seen as unprofessional.
- It would interrupt a critical flow or complex explanation.
