# Cascading Content Funnel

## What It Is

A distribution pattern that sequences a single core asset into a duration-graded release funnel: short-form hooks first, mid-length clips next, then the full-length core audio or video. Casual scrollers are stepped up through progressively longer formats until they arrive at the core asset as invested listeners.

## Best For

- Podcasts with video.
- Long-form interview and discussion shows.
- Programs with an active short-form social presence.
- Brands building a new audience from cold social traffic.
- Owned media programs that repurpose one recording into many cuts.

## Why It Works

Audience trust is not granted in a single step. A cold viewer will spend 60 seconds long before they commit 60 minutes.

The funnel respects that. A 60-second short earns the first unit of attention on a social platform. A 3-minute mid-length clip deepens the relationship and signals there is more substance behind the hook. The full-length core audio or video is the destination, reached only after the viewer has already agreed to spend time twice.

Each rung raises the commitment ceiling incrementally instead of asking for the full commitment up front. The result is a higher conversion rate from casual scroller to core listener than a single-format push, and a durable trust ramp that compounds across releases.

## Required Elements

- One core asset (60-minute audio or video) as the funnel destination.
- 60-second short-form cuts for cold social distribution.
- 3-minute mid-length clips as the intermediate rung.
- A clear escalation path (each shorter cut points toward the next longer format).
- Consistent branding across all three tiers so the audience recognizes the throughline.
- A release sequence that leads with shorts and steps up, not a simultaneous dump.

## Quality Bar

A viewer arriving from a 60-second short should be able to find and step up to the mid-length clip, and from there to the core asset, without friction. Each tier must stand on its own while pointing clearly to the next.

## When Not To Use

Avoid the cascading funnel when:

- There is no full-length core asset to funnel toward.
- The program has no short-form social distribution channel.
- The core asset does not yield clean, self-contained short and mid-length cuts.
- The team cannot sustain the repurposing volume across all three tiers.
