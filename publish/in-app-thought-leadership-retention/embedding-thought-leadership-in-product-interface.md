# Embedding Thought Leadership Within Product Interface

## What It Is

Embedding thought leadership videos, banners, and feature tours directly into the application experience rather than isolating them in marketing channels.

## Best For

- Product-led Growth (PLG) motions.
- Keeping customers aware of product advancement.
- Subtly introducing cross-sell opportunities without hard sales pressure.

## Why It Works

This pattern keeps customers engaged within the product interface itself, ensuring that valuable thought leadership content is consumed in context. By integrating these elements, it fosters continuous education and reinforces product value, leading to increased stickiness and organic cross-sell.

## Required Elements

- Strategically placed in-app content delivery mechanisms (e.g., banners, modals, embedded videos).
- Clear content strategy for thought leadership materials.
- Tracking and analytics to measure engagement with in-app content.

## Quality Bar

Thought leadership content must be seamlessly integrated and contextually relevant, enhancing the user experience without being intrusive. It should clearly communicate value and encourage further engagement within the product.

## When Not To Use

- When the in-app experience is already complex or crowded, risking information overload.
- For purely top-of-funnel awareness content that is not directly related to in-product value.
