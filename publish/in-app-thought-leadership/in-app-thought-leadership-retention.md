# Embedding Thought Leadership within Product Interface

## What It Is

In Product-Led Growth (PLG) motions, moving thought leadership videos, banners, and feature tours directly into the application experience (rather than isolating them in marketing channels) keeps customers aware of product advancement and subtly introduces cross-sell opportunities without hard sales pressure.

## Best For

- Product-Led Growth (PLG) motions.
- Increasing customer awareness of product advancement.
- Generating subtle cross-sell opportunities.

## Why It Works

This pattern keeps customers engaged within the product environment, leveraging their existing attention and providing context-rich information without disrupting their workflow by forcing them into external marketing channels. It also prevents valuable thought leadership content from being lost or overlooked in crowded external feeds.

## Required Elements

- Thought leadership content (e.g., videos, banners, interactive feature tours).
- A product interface capable of seamlessly embedding or displaying this content.
- A clear strategy for content placement to ensure visibility without being intrusive.
- A mechanism to track engagement with the embedded thought leadership.

## Quality Bar

The embedded content must be relevant, valuable, and seamlessly integrated into the product experience. It should enhance the user's understanding of the product and its value, rather than detracting from their core tasks. The overall experience should feel native and helpful, not like a forced advertisement.

## When Not To Use

- When the thought leadership content is not directly relevant to the product or the user's current context within the application.
- When the product interface is already crowded, and adding more content would negatively impact usability or user experience.
- If the content requires extensive external context, interaction, or dedicated focus that cannot be adequately provided within the in-app experience.
