# Lanyard Sponsorship Dominance

## What It Is

A strategy for B2B event marketing that prioritizes lanyard sponsorships over traditional booth space to achieve higher impressions at a lower cost.

## Best For

-   B2B events where broad brand impressions are key.
-   Programs with budget constraints seeking efficient visibility.
-   Situations where organic conversation generation is valued.

## Why It Works

Lanyards are ubiquitous at events, appearing in nearly every photo taken and serving as an automatic conversation starter for attendees and staff. This creates a pervasive brand presence that makes it appear as if your organization "owns" the conference, effectively cutting through the competition faced by traditional booths and other activations.

## Required Elements

-   Engagement with event organizers for exclusive lanyard sponsorship.
-   Clear branding and messaging on lanyards.
-   Alignment with overall event marketing strategy.

## Quality Bar

Achieve demonstrably higher brand impressions and organic interactions at a lower comparative cost than traditional B2B event marketing activations.

## When Not To Use

-   When the primary goal is in-depth product demonstrations requiring dedicated space.
-   For highly interactive activations that necessitate a physical presence.
-   When direct lead capture from a booth is a critical KPI.
