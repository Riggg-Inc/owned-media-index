# Live-Attendance Gating

## What It Is

A distribution tactic where high-value resources, bonuses, or giveaways are explicitly reserved for live attendees, while asynchronous viewers receive only the standard recording. The value split is stated up front so registrants know what they forfeit by watching later.

## Best For

- Webinars and virtual events.
- Live workshops and masterclasses.
- Launch or offer-driven sessions.
- Programs converting registrants into active real-time participants.

## Why It Works

Most virtual sessions leak attention to the recording: registrants assume they can catch up later, so live turnout drops. Gating the highest-value assets — a services giveaway, a readiness plan, a limited resource — to live attendance creates urgency and a clear reason to show up synchronously.

The mechanism is loss aversion plus scarcity. Everyone still gets the baseline recording, so no one is punished, but the marginal value of attending live is made explicit and concrete. This converts passive registrants into active, real-time audience members and lifts live-participation metrics.

## Required Elements

- A clear split: what async viewers get (recording) vs. what live attendees get (gated bonus).
- A genuinely high-value gated asset, not a throwaway.
- Explicit communication of the split in promotion and at session open.
- A fulfillment path that reliably delivers the gated asset to live attendees only.
- Baseline recording still delivered to all registrants.

## Quality Bar

Registrants should be able to state, before the session, exactly what they gain by attending live versus watching the recording — and the gated asset should be valuable enough that the trade-off is real.

## When Not To Use

- Evergreen or on-demand content where synchronous attendance carries no value.
- Sessions where the "bonus" is low-value and gating would feel manipulative.
- Compliance- or accessibility-sensitive contexts where withholding materials from async viewers is inappropriate.
- Programs whose primary goal is maximum reach rather than live conversion.
